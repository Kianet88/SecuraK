Our expertise

Practical security services, focused on outcomes.

From identifying weaknesses to planning incident recovery, our work is grounded in evidence, clear communication, and actionable recommendations.

01 / ASSESS

Vulnerability Assessment

Identify weaknesses across internal and external environments before they become larger problems.

What the engagement focuses on

Configuration issues, outdated software, exposed services, and access-control gaps. Findings are organized into a prioritized remediation plan so teams can address the most important risks first.

02 / TEST

Penetration Testing

Evaluate how security weaknesses could be combined into meaningful attack paths.

What the engagement focuses on

Authorized, scoped testing of potential exploitation paths, privilege escalation opportunities, and lateral-movement risks. Results explain the observed issues and recommended improvements.

03 / RESPOND

Incident Response

Bring structure to incident investigation, containment planning, recovery, and follow-up improvements.

What the engagement focuses on

Reviewing incident evidence, understanding likely impact, planning containment and recovery steps, and documenting lessons learned. Scope and response responsibilities are agreed for each engagement.
