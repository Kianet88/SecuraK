Cybersecurity Services

Assess

Building Toward a SaaS Platform

The MVP demonstrates the workflow today. The following stages describe the intended product direction; they are roadmap goals, not claims of features already deployed.

Current Stage

Demonstration MVP

Local demonstration, synthetic incidents, investigation views, risk assessment, recommended actions, simulated response workflow and report output.

Next Stage

Controlled Pilot SaaS

Hosted environment, customer accounts and organization separation, persistent incident records, real log ingestion and human-approved response controls.

Long-Term Vision

Full Incident Response Platform

Continuous detection, integrations, advanced analysis, governed containment and remediation, response verification and subscription-based service.
