PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

One workflow. A clearer view of the incident.

Incident Command is being developed around six stages of incident management.

01 / DETECT

Incident Detection

Organize suspicious activity and incident signals into a central view for investigation and review.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Evidence & Investigation

Bring together event context, timelines, and supporting evidence.

03 / ASSESS

Risk Assessment

Evaluate severity, consider potential impact, and organize recommendations.

04 / AUTHORIZE

Controlled Response

Structure response decisions around policies, authorization, and human oversight.

05 / RESPOND

Response Workflow

Track response decisions and planned activities through a documented incident process.

06 / VERIFY

Verification & Audit

Preserve incident history, document verification, and support reporting and review.

HOW IT WORKS

PRODUCT DIRECTION

Building toward a hosted platform.

SecuraK is being developed incrementally. The stages below outline the current product direction and intended future development.

CURRENT STAGE

Incident Command Foundation

Initial Incident Command experience focused on incident investigation, risk assessment, recommended actions, response workflows, and reporting.

NEXT STAGE

Controlled Pilot SaaS

Planned hosted environment, customer accounts, organization separation, persistent incident records, and human-approved response controls.

LONG-TERM VISION

Incident Response Platform

Future goals include expanded integrations, analysis capabilities, governed containment, remediation, and response verification.
