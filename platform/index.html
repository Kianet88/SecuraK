The SecuraK platform

Security incidents need more than an alert.

Teams need context, evidence, risk evaluation, informed decisions and a way to review what happened. SecuraK is being developed to organize these activities into a clearer, more consistent incident lifecycle.

Platform capabilities

One workflow. A clearer view of the incident.

SecuraK is being developed around the key stages of incident management, helping teams move from suspicious activity toward a documented and reviewed response.

01 / DETECT

Incident detection

Organize suspicious activity and incident signals into a central view for investigation and review.

02 / INVESTIGATE

Evidence & investigation

Bring together event context, timelines and supporting evidence to help teams understand what happened.

03 / ASSESS

Risk assessment

Evaluate incident severity, understand potential impact and organize recommendations for the next decision.

04 / AUTHORIZE

Controlled response

Structure response decisions around policies, authorization and appropriate human oversight.

05 / RESPOND

Response workflow

Track planned or simulated response activity and its outcomes through a documented incident process.

06 / VERIFY

Verification & audit

Record verification results, preserve incident history and support clear reporting and review.

Product direction

Building toward a hosted platform.

SecuraK is being developed incrementally. The current demonstration establishes workflow concepts; the following stages describe intended future development, not features currently deployed.

Current stage

Demonstration MVP

Local demonstration using synthetic incidents, investigation views, risk assessment, recommended actions, simulated response workflow and report output.

Next stage

Controlled pilot SaaS

Planned hosted environment, customer accounts and organization separation, persistent incident records, real log ingestion and human-approved response controls.

Long-term vision

Incident response platform

Longer-term goals include integrations, expanded analysis, governed containment and remediation, response verification and subscription-based service.
